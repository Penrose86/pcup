# 2.11 — Pit Lane Regulations

Drivers must enter and leave the pit lane safely and in accordance with the designated circuit markings.

## Pit Entry & Exit Lines

Drivers must not improperly cross the designated pit-entry or pit-exit line.

A confirmed pit-line infringement may result in a 5-second penalty.

Stewards may consider circumstances where a line is crossed to avoid an accident or because of circumstances genuinely outside the driver's reasonable control.

## Pit Exit

Drivers leaving the pits must remain aware of approaching traffic.

Drivers already on the circuit should recognise that a car leaving the pit lane may be restricted by the pit-exit line.

## Pit Lane Overtaking

Overtaking within the pit lane is permitted where it occurs naturally and can be completed safely.

Drivers must not deliberately make contact with another competitor or create an unsafe situation while attempting to gain a position in the pit lane.
