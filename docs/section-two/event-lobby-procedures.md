# 2.1 — Event & Lobby Procedures

All participating teams must confirm their competing drivers no later than 30 minutes before the scheduled race start.

Each team is responsible for ensuring its selected drivers are correctly registered, eligible to compete and ready for the event.

Drivers must be present in the appropriate Penrose Cup communication channels and ready to receive lobby instructions.

## Team Principal

Each team must have a Team Principal, who acts as the primary point of contact between the team and Penrose Cup Management.

## Race Captain

Each team must nominate a Race Captain for each event.

The Race Captain is responsible for race-night communication and assisting with lobby organisation where required.

The Team Principal and Race Captain may be the same person.

## Lobby Organisation

Official race lobbies will be organised by the designated Race Manager or authorised Penrose Cup Staff member.

Drivers must follow the joining procedure communicated for that event and should join promptly when instructed.

Competitors are responsible for ensuring that their game, platform and internet connection are ready before joining the lobby.
