# 2.3 — Late Drivers

The Penrose Cup will not normally delay an event because an individual competitor is late.

Where the game permits a late driver to join and Penrose Cup Staff authorise their participation, the driver may enter the session.

There is no guarantee that a late driver will be permitted to participate once the event has begun.

Driver registration and eligibility remain subject to the 30-minute registration deadline contained within the Team & Cup Regulations.
