# 2.15 — Classification & DNF

A driver who fails to finish a race may remain classified where they have completed at least 90% of the scheduled race distance.

A classified driver remains eligible to receive the finishing-position points applicable to their official classified position.

A driver who fails to meet the required classification threshold will be recorded as a DNF and will not receive finishing-position points.

A disqualified driver is not treated as a classified DNF.

The application of bonus points and team scoring is governed by the Team & Cup Regulations.
