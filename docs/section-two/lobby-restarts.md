# 2.4 — Lobby Restarts

An individual disconnection, technical problem or game issue does not automatically result in a lobby restart.

A restart is an exceptional measure and may only be authorised by the:

- Penrose Cup Owner
- Cup Director
- Designated Race Manager

Drivers and teams must not independently decide to restart, abandon or recreate an official session.

Where a significant lobby-wide technical problem occurs, Penrose Cup Management will determine the appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Drivers must continue competing unless instructed otherwise by an authorised official.
