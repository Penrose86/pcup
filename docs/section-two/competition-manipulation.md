# 2.17 — Competition Manipulation

Legitimate teamwork and team strategy are permitted within the Penrose Cup.

However, no driver or team may deliberately manipulate an event through [prohibited conduct](../section-three/race-manipulation.md).

This includes deliberately:

- Causing an accident for strategic benefit.
- Triggering a Safety Car, VSC or yellow flag.
- Using a lapped car to intentionally obstruct another team.
- Sacrificing a driver's race through deliberate contact to influence another team's result.
- Manipulating qualifying conditions.
- Exploiting a game mechanic to alter the outcome of an event unfairly.

Team instructions do not remove an individual driver's responsibility for their actions.

Where organised manipulation involves multiple members of a team, action may be taken against both the individual competitors and the team.
