# 2.19 — Authority During an Event

Competitors must follow reasonable organisational instructions issued by the designated Race Manager or authorised Penrose Cup Staff.

Drivers must not deliberately disrupt an official lobby, ignore instructions relating to the operation of the event or interfere with the orderly running of a session.

Sporting or organisational situations not expressly covered by these regulations may be determined by Penrose Cup Management where a decision is necessary for the event to continue.

Any such decision should, wherever reasonably possible, remain consistent with the established Penrose Cup regulations.
