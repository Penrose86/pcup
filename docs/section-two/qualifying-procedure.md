# 2.5 — Qualifying Procedure

The qualifying format used for an event will be determined by the applicable Penrose Cup competition format.

Where a Wildcard introduces an alternative qualifying requirement, the announced Wildcard conditions will apply.

## Flying Laps

Drivers completing a valid flying lap are entitled to make reasonable use of the circuit without being unnecessarily impeded by competitors who are not on a competitive lap.

## Out-Laps, In-Laps & Invalidated Laps

Drivers on an:

- Out-lap
- In-lap
- Invalidated lap
- Abandoned flying lap

must make a reasonable effort not to impede a driver completing a valid flying lap.

Drivers should use their mirrors, mini-map and other available information to remain aware of approaching traffic.

When allowing another car through, movements should be safe and predictable.

## Creating a Gap

Drivers may reduce their speed to create space before beginning a flying lap, provided this is done safely.

Drivers must not unnecessarily stop, drive dangerously slowly or create an unreasonable obstruction to other competitors.

Particular care should be taken where several drivers are attempting to create qualifying gaps in the same area.

## Qualifying Impeding

Unnecessarily interfering with another driver's flying lap may be reported to the stewards.

[Qualifying impeding offences](../section-three/qualifying-conduct.md) and their applicable penalties are dealt with under the Racing Standards and Stewarding Regulations.
