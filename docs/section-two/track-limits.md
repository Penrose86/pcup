# 2.10 — Track Limits & Corner Cutting

The game's track-limit system is the primary method of enforcing track limits during Penrose Cup events.

Drivers are expected to make a reasonable effort to remain within the circuit boundaries.

The absence of an in-game warning or penalty does not prevent the stewards from reviewing an incident where leaving the circuit materially affects another competitor or produces an unfair advantage.

Where an advantage is gained by leaving the circuit, voluntarily surrendering that advantage may be considered as mitigation but does not automatically prevent further stewarding action.

## Track-Limit Reviews

Where a driver requests the review or removal of an in-game track-limit warning or penalty, appropriate evidence from the game's [Race Director](../section-four/race-director-evidence.md) must be supplied.

For clarity, Race Director refers to the Race Director information within EA SPORTS F1 26 and not a Penrose Cup official.

A track-limit warning or penalty will not be removed without sufficient in-game Race Director evidence.
