# 2.6 — Race Start & Race Procedure

Drivers must be ready to begin the event when instructed by the Race Manager.

Where a formation lap is enabled, competitors must complete it in accordance with the game's procedure and the Penrose Cup regulations.

Drivers must not deliberately disrupt another competitor during the formation lap.

Once the race begins, the normal Penrose Cup Racing Standards apply immediately.

There is no separate set of Penrose Cup driving standards for Lap 1.

Drivers must continue racing unless instructed otherwise by authorised Penrose Cup Staff.
