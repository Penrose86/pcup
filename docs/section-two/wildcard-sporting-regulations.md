# 2.18 — Wildcard Sporting Regulations

Wildcard Rounds may operate from Week 3 onwards.

Where Penrose Cup Management activates a Wildcard, the announced conditions become part of the Sporting Regulations for that event.

Wildcard conditions may alter matters such as:

- Weather conditions.
- Available tyre compounds.
- Mandatory tyre requirements.
- Qualifying tyre requirements.
- Qualifying format.
- Strategy requirements.
- Other specifically announced sporting conditions.

All affected teams and drivers will be informed of the applicable Wildcard requirements in advance.

Unless a rule is specifically changed by the Wildcard announcement, all normal Penrose Cup Sporting Regulations, Racing Standards and Stewarding Regulations remain in force.
