# 2.14 — Disconnects & AI-Controlled Cars

A driver who disconnects may attempt to rejoin the session where the game permits them to do so.

An individual disconnection does not automatically result in a restart.

Where a disconnected driver's car becomes AI-controlled, competitors should continue racing normally.

Where an AI-controlled car causes or materially contributes to an incident, affected competitors may submit the incident for steward review.

Widespread lobby or connection failures will be handled under the Lobby Restart procedure.
