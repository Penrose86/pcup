# 2.13 — Retiring From a Session

A driver wishing to voluntarily retire from qualifying or a race should return to the pit lane and retire safely.

Drivers must not deliberately stop on or beside the circuit for the purpose of retiring.

A driver must not deliberately retire in a manner capable of creating or influencing:

- Yellow flags.
- Safety Car conditions.
- Virtual Safety Car conditions.
- Another driver's qualifying lap.
- Another competitor's race.

Where a damaged car remains capable of returning safely to the pits, the driver should make a reasonable effort to do so.
