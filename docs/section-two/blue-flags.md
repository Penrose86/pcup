# 2.9 — Blue Flags & Lapped Cars

A driver receiving blue flags must allow an approaching lapping car to pass at the earliest safe opportunity.

Drivers must not ignore three consecutive blue-flag warnings.

A lapped driver should remain predictable and avoid unnecessarily interfering with cars on the lead lap.

A driver who legitimately has sufficient pace to unlap themselves may do so through normal racing, provided the move is completed safely.

Deliberate tactical blocking or interference by a lapped driver is covered by the Racing Standards.
