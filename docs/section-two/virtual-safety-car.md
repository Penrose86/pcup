# 2.8 — Virtual Safety Car Procedure

During a Virtual Safety Car, all competitors must comply with the game's VSC delta system.

Drivers must keep their delta within the permitted three-second delta window.

For clarity, this refers to the driver's in-game VSC delta.

It does not mean a driver must remain three seconds behind the car in front.

Drivers must not deliberately build an excessive positive delta with the intention of gaining an advantage when the VSC period ends.

Overtaking remains prohibited while VSC conditions apply except where the game legitimately permits a position change.

Deliberate VSC manipulation may be referred to the stewards under the [VSC Driving Standards](../section-three/vsc-driving.md).
