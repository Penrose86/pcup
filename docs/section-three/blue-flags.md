# 3.12 — Blue Flags

A lapped driver must allow an approaching lead-lap car through at the earliest safe opportunity.

Drivers must not ignore three consecutive blue-flag warnings.

Lapped drivers should remain predictable and avoid unnecessary interference with cars competing on the lead lap.

A lapped driver must not deliberately obstruct another competitor for tactical reasons or to benefit a teammate.

Where a lapped driver legitimately has sufficient pace to unlap themselves, they may do so through normal and safe racing.
