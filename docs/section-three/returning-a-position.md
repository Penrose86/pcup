# 3.7 — Returning a Position

A driver who believes they have gained an unfair position or advantage may voluntarily return it.

Returning a position may be considered as [mitigation](../section-four/mitigation.md) by the stewards.

It does not automatically cancel the original offence.

Causing another driver to spin and subsequently returning the position does not necessarily restore the time, positions, tyre condition or race situation lost by the affected driver.

The original incident may therefore still receive a penalty.
