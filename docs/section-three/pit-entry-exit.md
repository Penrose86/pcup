# 3.16 — Pit Entry & Exit Conduct

Drivers entering or leaving the pit lane must do so safely and predictably.

Crossing a designated pit-entry or pit-exit line may result in a 5-second penalty.

Drivers leaving the pits should remain aware of approaching traffic, while drivers on the circuit should recognise that an exiting car may be restricted by the pit-exit line.

Clean and safe overtaking within the pit lane is permitted.
