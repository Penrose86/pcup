# 3.2 — Racing Room & Cars Alongside

Drivers must leave appropriate racing room when another car has established a legitimate position alongside.

Neither attacking nor defending drivers may deliberately force an entitled competitor beyond the edge of the circuit.

A small or extremely late overlap does not automatically establish an entitlement to racing room.

Stewards will consider when and how the overlap was established, the position of both cars, the braking phase, whether the attacking driver remained in control and whether the corner could reasonably have been completed on the circuit.
