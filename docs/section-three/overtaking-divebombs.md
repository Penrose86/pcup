# 3.3 — Overtaking & Divebombs

A driver attempting an overtake must be sufficiently in control of their car to make the move safely.

Excessively late or uncontrolled lunges that result in contact, force another driver off track, cause damage, or result in another driver losing time or positions may be penalised.

Simply reaching the inside of another car after braking excessively late does not automatically establish a right to racing room.

Stewards may consider whether the attacking driver:

- Established a reasonable position before or during the normal braking phase.
- Maintained reasonable control.
- Was capable of making the corner.
- Remained within reasonable circuit boundaries.
- Caused contact or forced the defending driver from the circuit.
