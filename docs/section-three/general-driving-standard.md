# 3.1 — General Driving Standard

Drivers must make a reasonable effort to maintain control of their car and avoid unnecessary contact.

Competitive racing and reasonable mistakes are part of racing.

Contact does not automatically mean that a penalty will be issued.

When reviewing an incident, stewards may consider:

- The actions of all drivers involved.
- Avoidability.
- Position and trajectory.
- Available racing room.
- Control of the car.
- Damage.
- Time or positions lost.
- Whether a driver was spun or forced from the circuit.
- The overall effect of the incident.

The consequences of an incident may affect the severity of any resulting penalty.
