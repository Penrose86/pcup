# 3.14 — Safety Car Driving Standards

Drivers must behave predictably while operating behind the Safety Car.

Once the Safety Car lights are extinguished, the race leader controls the pace of the field.

The leader may control the pace before committing to the restart.

However, once the leader accelerates decisively to restart the race, they must not deliberately brake or significantly slow again in an attempt to restart the procedure for a second time.

Drivers behind remain responsible for avoiding unnecessary contact.

Deliberate Safety Car manipulation may receive serious penalties.
