# 3.13 — Qualifying Conduct

Drivers not on a competitive qualifying lap must make a reasonable effort not to impede drivers on flying laps.

This applies to drivers on:

- Out-laps.
- In-laps.
- Invalidated laps.
- Abandoned flying laps.

Movement to allow a faster driver through must be safe and predictable.

A confirmed qualifying impeding offence may result in a [5–15 second race-time penalty](../section-four/qualifying-impeding.md), depending on severity and impact.

More serious or repeated offences may result in a qualifying ban at the driver's next applicable event.
