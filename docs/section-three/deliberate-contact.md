# 3.18 — Deliberate Contact & Retaliation

Deliberate contact is strictly prohibited.

Drivers must not retaliate against another competitor because of an earlier collision, disagreement, report or stewarding decision.

Examples include:

- Deliberately driving into another car.
- Brake-checking another competitor.
- Waiting for another driver to interfere with them.
- Attempting to damage another competitor's car.
- Causing a collision in retaliation.

Deliberate or retaliatory incidents may be treated as serious offences and may receive sanctions significantly stronger than those used for normal racing incidents.
