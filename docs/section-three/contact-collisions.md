# 3.6 — Contact & Collisions

Drivers must make a reasonable effort to avoid contact.

Not every collision will result in a penalty.

For guidance, incidents may broadly be considered:

## Minor

Little or no meaningful time or position loss and little or no damage.

## Moderate

A spin, significant loss of time or positions, or notable damage.

## Major

Severe or terminal damage, retirement from the event, substantial consequences or particularly reckless driving.

These categories are guidance only and do not create automatic penalties.
