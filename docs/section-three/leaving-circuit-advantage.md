# 3.9 — Leaving the Circuit & Gaining an Advantage

Drivers must make a reasonable effort to remain within the circuit boundaries.

A driver leaving the circuit must not gain a lasting advantage through doing so.

Where an advantage is gained, voluntarily surrendering that advantage may be considered as mitigation.

Additional steward action may still be taken where leaving the circuit:

- Results in an unfair overtake.
- Prevents another driver completing an overtake.
- Causes another driver to lose time or position.
- Results in contact or damage.
- Is deliberate or reckless.
