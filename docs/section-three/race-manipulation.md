# 3.19 — Race Manipulation

Legitimate teamwork and strategy are permitted.

However, drivers must not use deliberate incidents or prohibited behaviour to manipulate the outcome of an event.

This includes:

- Deliberately causing a Safety Car or VSC.
- Creating yellow flags for strategic benefit.
- Crashing into an opponent to assist a teammate.
- Using a lapped car to obstruct another team.
- Sacrificing a driver's own race to deliberately interfere with an opponent.
- Exploiting an unintended game mechanic to manipulate an event.

Where evidence demonstrates organised manipulation involving multiple members of a team, action may be considered against both the individual drivers and the team.
