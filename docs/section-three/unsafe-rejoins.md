# 3.10 — Unsafe Rejoins

A driver who leaves the circuit must rejoin safely.

The returning driver has primary responsibility for ensuring that their rejoin does not create an unreasonable danger to approaching traffic.

Drivers must not immediately rejoin onto the racing line where doing so forces another competitor into significant avoiding action.

Where necessary, a driver should wait for approaching traffic before returning to the circuit.

Unsafe rejoins causing contact, damage, a spin or significant loss of time or position may receive stronger penalties.
