# 5.7 DNS & Disqualification

### Did Not Start — DNS

A driver who does not start the race receives **zero points**.

Where a DNS results in a team fielding fewer than four eligible drivers, the team may also receive the appropriate administrative infringement under the **Warning & Reprimand System**.

### Disqualification — DSQ

A driver disqualified from an event receives:

- **Zero finishing-position points**
- **No Pole Position bonus**
- **No Fastest Lap bonus**
- **No Cleanest Driver bonus**

Any additional stewarding or competition sanction remains separate from the loss of points.
