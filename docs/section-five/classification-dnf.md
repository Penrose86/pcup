# 5.6 Classification & DNF

A driver must complete at least **90% of the scheduled race distance** to be classified.

A driver who retires or disconnects after completing at least 90% of the race distance may still be classified by the game and receive the finishing-position points corresponding to their final classified position.

A driver who completes **less than 90%** of the race distance is unclassified and receives **no finishing-position points**.

The separate regulations governing safe retirement, disconnects and AI-controlled cars continue to apply.
