# 5.10 — Classification & Points Eligibility

A driver must complete at least 90% of the scheduled race distance to be classified.

A driver who fails to finish the race, including through a disconnection or DNF, may still score finishing-position points where they have completed at least 90% of the scheduled race distance and remain officially classified.

A classified DNF or disconnected driver receives the points associated with their final classified position.

A driver who completes less than 90% of the scheduled race distance is unclassified and receives no finishing-position points.

## DNS

DNS — Did Not Start

A driver recorded as DNS receives:

0 points.

## DSQ

DSQ — Disqualified

A disqualified driver receives:

0 finishing-position points.

A disqualified driver also loses all bonus points from that race.

This includes any:

- Pole Position bonus.
- Fastest Lap bonus.
- Cleanest Driver bonus.
