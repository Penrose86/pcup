# 5.12 Management Discretion

It is impossible for a competition rulebook to anticipate every technical failure, game issue or exceptional circumstance.

Where a situation occurs that is not specifically addressed by these regulations, **Penrose Cup Management may make a reasonable decision necessary to allow the competition to continue fairly**.

Management discretion is intended for exceptional competition, administrative or operational circumstances.

It does not permit routine alteration of the established **Racing Standards or Stewarding Regulations**.

Any decision made under this provision should, wherever reasonably possible, preserve fair and equal treatment between participating teams.
