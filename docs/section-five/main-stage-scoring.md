# 5.12 — Main Stage Scoring

Weeks 3 and 4 of the Penrose Cup form the Main Stage.

The Main Stage uses a separate team-placement points system.

This system is used because the Main Stage is conducted across three separate lobbies and the number of active or classified drivers may not always be identical between those lobbies.

Normal Penrose Cup driver points are still calculated within each lobby.

However, those driver points are used to determine the finishing order of the five teams within that lobby.

Once each team's position has been established, Main Stage team points are awarded as follows:

| Team Position | Main Stage Points |
|---|---:|
| 1st Team | 5 |
| 2nd Team | 4 |
| 3rd Team | 3 |
| 4th Team | 2 |
| 5th Team | 1 |

> **INFO:** **The Penrose Cup uses two different scoring methods.**
>
> **STANDARD SCORING**
>
> The 33-to-1 points system is the normal driver scoring system. It is used to score individual drivers and, during normal scoring stages, contributes directly towards the team's race score.
>
> **MAIN STAGE SCORING**
>
> During Weeks 3 and 4, normal driver points are first used to establish each team's position within its individual lobby. Teams then receive 5-to-1 Main Stage points according to that team finishing position.
>
> It is the 5-to-1 Main Stage points that determine the overall Main Stage standings and progression to the Semi-Final.

For clarity:

Normal 33-to-1 driver points determine the team finishing order within each Main Stage lobby.

The resulting 5-to-1 team-placement points are what count towards the overall Main Stage standings.
