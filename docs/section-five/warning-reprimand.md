# 5.2 Warning & Reprimand System

The Warning & Reprimand System applies to **administrative and team-related infringements** and is separate from normal on-track stewarding penalties.

A team's:

**First administrative infringement = Warning**

Every subsequent administrative infringement = **Reprimand**

Reprimands accumulate throughout the entire Penrose Cup competition and **do not reset between rounds**.

### Reaching Five Reprimands

When a team receives its **fifth Reprimand**, the team's highest-scoring driver during that race week will lose **50% of the points they scored during that race week**.

After a team has reached five Reprimands, any subsequent race week in which that team receives another Reprimand will trigger the same 50% points deduction.

Only **one 50% deduction per team per race week** may be applied, regardless of how many Reprimands the team receives during that week.

Reprimands continue to accumulate numerically.

For example, if a team begins a race week with six Reprimands and receives Reprimands seven, eight and nine during that week, only **one 50% deduction** is applied.

If the team then receives its tenth Reprimand during the following race week, another 50% deduction will apply for that week.
