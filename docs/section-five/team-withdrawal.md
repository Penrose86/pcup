# 5.9 Team Withdrawal

A registered team may withdraw from the Penrose Cup at any stage.

Once the official competition draw has been completed, **Penrose Cup Management will not recruit or introduce a new outside team to replace a withdrawing team**.

If a team withdraws during a competition stage, the remaining teams will continue.

For example, if Round Two begins with 15 teams and one team withdraws, the remaining 14 teams will continue competing for the five Semi-Final positions.

A further withdrawal would result in the remaining 13 teams continuing.

### Existing Results

Any points, results and classifications achieved by a team **before its withdrawal remain valid**.

Completed results will not be retrospectively removed or recalculated because that team subsequently withdraws.
