# SECTION FIVE — TEAM & CUP REGULATIONS

This section governs the team responsibilities and competition administration that apply throughout the Penrose Cup.

It covers team identity, administrative infringements, driver attendance, race points, classification, countback, withdrawals and Penrose Cup Management discretion.

Teams are responsible for ensuring their drivers are correctly prepared and able to meet their competition commitments. The regulations below also explain how points are awarded, when a driver is classified, and how ties or vacant qualification places are handled.
