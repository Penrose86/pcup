# 5.1 Team Liveries & Badges

Every participating driver must use their team's **registered matching F1 World Car livery and registered matching team badge**.

Race suits are **not regulated**.

The Team Principal is responsible for ensuring that all participating drivers, including any drivers who may be required as backups, are correctly prepared before the event.

If a driver enters an official race using an incorrect team livery or badge, the driver will be permitted to continue racing.

The team will receive the appropriate administrative infringement under the **Warning & Reprimand System**.
