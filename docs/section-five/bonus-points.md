# 5.5 Bonus Points

Two additional bonus points are available during a standard Penrose Cup race:

- **Pole Position — +1 point**
- **Fastest Lap — +1 point**

The Fastest Lap bonus is awarded regardless of the driver's finishing position, provided the result remains valid under the regulations.

There is **no Cleanest Driver, Driver of the Day or Most Overtakes bonus**.

A race winner who also takes Pole Position and Fastest Lap can therefore score a maximum of:

**33 + 1 + 1 = 35 points.**
