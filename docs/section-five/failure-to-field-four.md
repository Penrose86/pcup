# 5.3 Failure to Field Four Drivers

A standard Penrose Cup event requires each team to field **four eligible drivers**.

A team may still participate if it can only field one, two or three drivers.

Failure to field four eligible drivers constitutes **one administrative infringement per event**.

The infringement remains a single infringement regardless of whether the team fields three, two or one driver.

The appropriate **Warning or Reprimand** will be issued under Section 5.2.

Any eligible drivers who do participate may continue to score points normally.
