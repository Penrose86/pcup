# 4.4 — In-Game Race Director Evidence

For the purposes of Penrose Cup stewarding, Race Director refers to the Race Director information provided within EA SPORTS F1 26.

It does not refer to a Penrose Cup staff position.

Race Director evidence may be required where information regarding warnings, penalties, collisions, track limits or other recorded events is relevant.

Appropriate Race Director evidence is required when requesting the removal or review of an in-game track-limit warning or penalty.
