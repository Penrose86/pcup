# 4.13 — Discussion of Active Tickets

Competitors may discuss active stewarding tickets.

However, discussion must remain respectful.

Disagreement and debate are permitted.

Personal insults, bullying, harassment or abuse directed at drivers, stewards or staff are not.

Penrose Cup Staff may intervene where discussion becomes abusive or disruptive.

This may include temporarily restricting a person's ability to participate in the relevant communication channels.
