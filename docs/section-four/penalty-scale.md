# 4.6 — Penalty Scale

The standard Penrose Cup stewarding penalty scale is:

No Further Action

↓

Warning

↓

5 Seconds

↓

10 Seconds

↓

15 Seconds

↓

20+ Seconds

↓

Qualifying Ban

↓

Event Disqualification

↓

Competition Disqualification

This is not a mandatory progressive ladder.

A serious offence may immediately receive a stronger sanction without the driver first receiving lower-level penalties.

Steward-issued race-time penalties begin at 5 seconds and normally increase in multiples of five seconds.

Penrose Cup stewards do not issue 3-second stewarding penalties.

## Converted DNF Time Penalties – Team Point Deductions

Where a driver receives a time penalty but subsequently records a DNF, the outstanding time penalty will be converted into a deduction from their team's championship points.

| Original Time Penalty | Team Points Deduction |
| --- | --- |
| 5 seconds | -2 points |
| 10 seconds | -4 to -5 points |
| 15 seconds | -7 to -10 points |
| 20 seconds | -13 points or more |
| More severe penalties | -15 points or more |

For more severe incidents, the deduction will be determined following discussion between the Head Steward and Admins.

The severity and circumstances of each incident will be considered when determining the final points deduction.

These deductions apply to the team's championship total and are separate from race-time penalties.
