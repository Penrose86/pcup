# 4.6 — Penalty Scale

The standard Penrose Cup stewarding penalty scale is:

No Further Action

↓

Warning

↓

5 Seconds

↓

10 Seconds

↓

15 Seconds

↓

20+ Seconds

↓

Qualifying Ban

↓

Event Disqualification

↓

Competition Disqualification

This is not a mandatory progressive ladder.

A serious offence may immediately receive a stronger sanction without the driver first receiving lower-level penalties.

Steward-issued race-time penalties begin at 5 seconds and normally increase in multiples of five seconds.

Penrose Cup stewards do not issue 3-second stewarding penalties.
