# 4.7 — Incident Severity

When determining an appropriate sanction, stewards may consider:

- Responsibility.
- Avoidability.
- Recklessness.
- Damage caused.
- Time lost.
- Positions lost.
- Whether another driver spun.
- Whether another driver retired.
- Whether the incident significantly altered another competitor's race.
- Whether the behaviour was deliberate.
- Any relevant mitigating circumstances.

The result of an incident is relevant but will not necessarily be the only factor considered.
