# 1.2 — Competition Format

The Penrose Cup is designed around a progressive competition format, beginning with a Group Stage and ultimately leading to the Main Stage, Semi-Final and Grand Final.

The competition is initially designed around approximately 20–30 participating teams. However, the final structure of each Penrose Cup will be determined by the level of demand and the number of confirmed entries.

Where participation is significantly higher than anticipated, Penrose Cup Management may expand or adapt the competition structure to accommodate additional teams. This may include additional groups, qualification stages, divisions or other suitable competition structures.

Any expanded format will be designed to provide teams with a fair and meaningful competition and will be announced before the relevant competition begins.

For the standard format, the competition will be reduced to 15 teams for the Main Stage, followed by five teams in the Semi-Final and two teams in the Grand Final.

Adjustments to the competition structure are administrative and do not alter the Penrose Cup Sporting Regulations, Racing Standards or Stewarding Regulations.
