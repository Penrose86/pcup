# 1.2 — Competition Format

The Penrose Cup is a six-week team competition played on EA SPORTS F1 26. Each registered team competes with four drivers per event.

Week 1 is the **Placement Round**. Its purpose is to assess the relative ability of the competing teams and divide the field into two competition groups:

- **Group A — Higher Ability**
- **Group B — Lower Ability**

No team is eliminated during the Placement Round. The method for calculating placement results across multiple lobbies will be published once confirmed.

Following placement, Group A and Group B operate as separate competitions, using the same sporting regulations, racing standards, stewarding procedures and points system. Each group progresses independently through Group Race 1, Group Race 2, Knockout, Semi-Final and Final.

The objective is for five teams from each group to reach Week 6. Each Group Final therefore consists of five teams and 20 drivers. The winning team of each Final is crowned champion of its respective group.

The exact number of eliminations depends on the number of teams competing in each group.

### 15 Teams per Group

| Week | Round | Teams | Progression |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Placement Round | 15 | Placement only — no elimination |
| 2 | Group Race 1 | 15 | No elimination |
| 3 | Group Race 2 | 15 → 10 | 5 teams eliminated |
| 4 | Knockout | 10 → 7 | 3 teams eliminated |
| 5 | Semi-Final | 7 → 5 | 2 teams eliminated |
| 6 | Final | 5 | Group Championship decided |

### 10 Teams per Group

| Week | Round | Teams | Progression |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Placement Round | 10 | Placement only — no elimination |
| 2 | Group Race 1 | 10 | No elimination |
| 3 | Group Race 2 | 10 → 8 | 2 teams eliminated |
| 4 | Knockout | 8 → 6 | 2 teams eliminated |
| 5 | Semi-Final | 6 → 5 | 1 team eliminated |
| 6 | Final | 5 | Group Championship decided |

Where the confirmed entry produces a different number of teams per group, Management will publish the progression structure before the competition begins. The fundamental format remains Placement Round → Group Race 1 → Group Race 2 → Knockout → Semi-Final → five-team Final.

Adjustments to the competition structure are administrative and do not alter the Penrose Cup Sporting Regulations, Racing Standards or Stewarding Regulations.
