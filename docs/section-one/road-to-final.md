# 1.4 — Road to the Final

The Penrose Cup runs across six weeks. Following the Placement Round, Group A and Group B progress independently through the same stages. Each group aims to reduce its field to five teams for its Week 6 Final.

## Week 1 — Placement Round

All teams compete in the Placement Round. Results determine the allocation of teams to Group A and Group B. No team is eliminated. The method for combining results across multiple lobbies remains to be confirmed and will be published before the competition begins.

## Week 2 — Group Race 1

Group A and Group B compete separately. No teams are eliminated.

## Week 3 — Group Race 2

Group A and Group B compete separately. The number of teams eliminated from each group depends on its entry size and published progression structure.

## Week 4 — Knockout

Each group holds its Knockout round. Team numbers are reduced according to the applicable progression structure.

## Week 5 — Semi-Final

Each group holds its Semi-Final. Five teams from each group progress to Week 6.

## Week 6 — Group Finals

Each Group Final consists of five teams, with each team fielding four drivers (20 drivers per Final). The winning team in each race is crowned champion of its group.

The detailed team progression for groups of 15 or 10 teams is set out in [Competition Format](competition-format.md). Where group sizes differ, Management will publish the progression structure before the competition begins.
