# 1.4 — Road to the Final

The Penrose Cup is intended to run across a six-week competition, progressively reducing the number of teams until two remain for the Grand Final.

## Weeks 1–2 — Opening Group Stage

Teams will be drawn into groups, generally consisting of five teams.

Teams will compete in two Group Stage races, with results across both events determining progression.

The Group Stage will reduce the competition to 15 teams for the Main Stage.

## Weeks 3–4 — Main Stage

The remaining 15 teams will compete across three separate five-team lobbies each week.

Race allocations will be structured to provide balanced grids and maximise the number of different opponents each team faces, with repeat matchups kept to the minimum reasonably possible.

Teams receive Main Stage points based on their team finishing position within their lobby:

| Team Position | Main Stage Points |
|---|---:|
| 1st | 5 |
| 2nd | 4 |
| 3rd | 3 |
| 4th | 2 |
| 5th | 1 |

After both Main Stage rounds, the top five teams overall progress to the Semi-Final.

## Week 5 — Semi-Final

Five teams compete together in a full 20-driver race, with each team fielding four drivers.

Normal Penrose Cup scoring applies.

The top two teams progress to the Grand Final.

## Week 6 — Grand Final

The final two teams compete 10 vs 10 across two races on two different circuits.

Race One is a Sprint using One-Shot Qualifying and half finishing-position points. Bonus points remain at their full value.

Race Two is the Main Event using Short Qualifying followed by a 50% race with full Penrose Cup points.

Points from both races are combined to determine the final standings.

If the teams finish level on points, the standard Penrose Cup countback procedure applies.
