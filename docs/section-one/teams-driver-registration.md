# 1.4 — Teams & Driver Registration

Each team entering the Penrose Cup must register a minimum of four drivers.

There is no maximum number of drivers that a team may register for the competition.

For standard Penrose Cup events, each team may select any four eligible drivers from its registered roster.

Drivers are not required to be designated as reserves or substitutes. Any properly registered team driver may be selected for an event.

## Driver Eligibility

To compete and score points, a driver must:

- Be registered to the team they are representing.
- Be present in the Penrose Cup Discord and hold the appropriate roles.
- Complete any required race check-in.
- Meet the registration deadline of 30 minutes before the scheduled race start.

A driver registered after this deadline may only compete with permission from Penrose Cup Staff, and any points scored by that driver will not count towards their team.

## Team Responsibility

Teams are responsible for ensuring their selected drivers are correctly registered and eligible before each event.

A team may compete with fewer than four drivers.

However, failure to field four eligible drivers constitutes one team infringement for that event, regardless of whether the team fields one, two or three drivers.

## Driver Transfers

Drivers may transfer between registered teams where both teams agree.

Unusual or disputed circumstances may be referred to Penrose Cup Management.

A driver may represent only one team during any competition race week.

Once a driver has competed for a team during that race week, they cannot compete for another team until the following race week.

A transferred driver must be correctly registered to their new team before becoming eligible to compete for them.
