# 1.1 — Welcome

Welcome to the Penrose Cup, a team-based competition held on EA SPORTS F1 26.

The Penrose Cup brings teams together in a progressive competition where success is determined not only by individual driver performance, but by the combined performance of the entire team.

Teams will compete through multiple stages of the competition, beginning with the Group Stage and progressing through the Main Stage, Semi-Final, and ultimately the Grand Final.

All competitors are expected to race fairly, respect fellow competitors and officials, and follow the Sporting Regulations, Racing Standards and Stewarding Regulations throughout the competition.

Participation in a Penrose Cup event constitutes acceptance of the regulations contained within this rulebook.
