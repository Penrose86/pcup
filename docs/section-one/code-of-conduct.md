# 1.7 — Code of Conduct

All drivers, team personnel and staff participating in the Penrose Cup are expected to conduct themselves in a respectful and sporting manner.

This applies throughout official Penrose Cup activities, including race events, Discord channels, voice communications, incident reports and interactions with Penrose Cup Staff.

Competitors must not engage in:

- Personal abuse, harassment, bullying or discriminatory behaviour.
- Threatening or deliberately aggressive behaviour towards another competitor or member of staff.
- Deliberate disruption of Penrose Cup events or communications.
- Abusive behaviour towards stewards because of a decision or penalty.
- Knowingly submitting false or malicious incident reports.
- Repeated behaviour intended to create unnecessary conflict or disruption.

Drivers and teams are free to disagree with stewarding decisions and discuss them reasonably.

Where a competitor disagrees with an official stewarding outcome, the Penrose Cup [appeal process](../section-four/appeals.md) should be used.

Competitors must follow reasonable instructions issued by Penrose Cup Staff relating to the organisation and running of an event.

Breaches of the Code of Conduct may result in action appropriate to the seriousness of the offence, including warnings, restrictions, removal from an event or, in serious or repeated cases, removal from the Penrose Cup.
