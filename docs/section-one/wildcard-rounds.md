# 1.6 — Wildcard Rounds

Wildcard Rounds may be introduced from Week 3 onwards, including the Main Stage, Semi-Final and Grand Final.

The decision to activate a Wildcard Round will be made by Penrose Cup Management and Staff.

There is no requirement for a Wildcard to be used every week.

Where a Wildcard is activated, Management and Staff will determine the specific conditions applying to that event.

All affected teams and drivers will be informed of the Wildcard and its requirements in advance of the race.

Wildcard conditions may include, but are not limited to:

- Wet or predetermined weather conditions.
- Changeable weather conditions.
- Tyre restrictions, such as prohibiting the use of the Hard compound.
- Qualifying tyre requirements, such as requiring drivers to qualify on the Hard compound.
- Mandatory strategy requirements.
- Other special race or qualifying conditions determined by Penrose Cup Management and Staff.

Once announced, the Wildcard conditions form part of the regulations for that event.

The purpose of Wildcard Rounds is to introduce an additional strategic challenge during the later stages of the Penrose Cup while maintaining the same underlying Racing Standards and Stewarding Regulations.
